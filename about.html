About MJ Moving Company

A family-owned Greater Boston moving company built by someone who has spent a career getting trucks to the right door on time.

Founder · Mike Bourque

Movers who care

Mike Bourque has more than 25 years in moving and logistics, including years running day-to-day operations for a busy multi-truck moving company west of Boston. He started MJ Moving Company to run moves the way he always wanted them run: answer every call, show up when promised, and treat a customer's home and belongings like his own.

That is why the phone is answered 24 hours a day. People need movers at strange hours. A closing gets pushed, a lease ends early, a family member needs help this weekend. We pick up and make a plan.

We also keep the whole job under one roof. Packing, moving, junk removal, storage and TV mounting come from the same company, so you deal with one crew and one point of contact from start to finish.

Our standard on every job: protect the home, wrap the furniture, place every item where you want it, and leave nothing behind.
