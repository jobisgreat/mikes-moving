Common questions

What can't you take?

Hazardous materials such as paint, propane tanks, gasoline, chemicals and asbestos.

Can you haul junk on the same day as my move?

Yes. The same truck can take your leftovers so you don't need a second company.

Can you move my things and haul away junk the same day?

Yes. The same crew and truck can take furniture and clutter you don't want, so you don't need to book a second company.

What won't you move?

Hazardous items such as propane, gasoline, paint and chemicals. Keep cash, jewelry, medications and important documents with you.
