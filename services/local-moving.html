Common questions

Do you move on weekends and at night?

Yes. We schedule moves seven days a week, including early mornings and evenings, and we answer the phone 24 hours a day.

Can you move a single item?

Yes. Call with what it is and where it is going and we will quote it.

How far ahead should I book?

Three to four weeks is comfortable for most moves. For the end of the month, and especially September 1st, book as early as you can. September 1st is the busiest moving day in Boston.

Do you handle parking permits for the truck?

Boston, Cambridge and several nearby cities require reserved curb space for moving trucks. We help you apply or handle the application for you.

Do you take apart and reassemble furniture?

Yes. Bed frames, tables, desks and similar pieces are taken apart, moved and put back together.

What if something gets damaged?

Tell us right away and file a claim online or by phone. We investigate every claim and respond in writing.
