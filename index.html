    Home / File a claim

    File a moving claim or complaint

    If something arrived damaged, went missing, or the service fell short, tell us. We will look into it and answer you in writing.

    Start a claim

    How we protect your things

    Every move starts with protection: furniture is wrapped in moving pads, fragile items are packed and cushioned, and the truck is loaded so nothing shifts in transit. If you let us know about fragile, antique or high-value pieces before move day, we plan extra packing and handling for them.

    Even with careful work, accidents can happen. When they do, we want the chance to investigate and make it right.

    How to file

    1. Document the problem

      Take clear photos of the damaged item and any box or packing it came in. Keep the packing materials until the claim is settled if you can.

    2. Submit the claim form

      List each item, what happened, and an estimated value. Attach your photos. You can also call 508-215-6322 and we will start it with you.

    3. Watch for our confirmation

      You will get an email confirming we received your claim. Check your spam or junk folder if you don't see it.

    4. We investigate

      We review the move records and talk with the crew. We may ask to inspect the item or for a repair estimate.

    5. You get a written answer

      We send our decision and the resolution in writing, whether that is repair, replacement or payment.

    Deadlines

    File as soon as you notice a problem, and no later than the claim deadline printed on your Bill of Lading. Claims are easiest to resolve while the move is fresh and the packing is still on hand.

    Complaints about service

    Not every problem is a broken item. If you had an issue with scheduling, billing or how the crew treated you, use the same form and choose Service complaint. It goes straight to Mike.

    Have these ready

    • Your name and move date
    • Bill of Lading or job number
    • Photos of each damaged item
    • A rough value or repair estimate
