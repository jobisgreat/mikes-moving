File a claim

File a claim for loss or damage with Mikes Moving LLC. Mike handles the claim. Phone 508-215-6322. Email claims@mikesmoving.com. Billing disputes and crew complaints go to the same email. This form is for loss and damage.

What happens next

You get a claim number on this page. The same number is in the email. A written decision follows within 90 days. “Received” is not a decision.

The move is paid in full before the work. A claim is not reviewed until that invoice is paid in full.

The estimate includes Full Value Protection. A covered claim pays at full value: repair, replace, or cash at current value. It does not pay by weight. Valuation is not insurance.

Nothing on this page is excluded.

Do not discard a damaged item until the claim is closed in writing.

False or incomplete information can void the claim in part or in full. Mikes Moving LLC may ask for a notarized statement.

If we do not resolve an interstate claim, file with the FMCSA National Consumer Complaint Database. A Massachusetts claim can also go to the Massachusetts Attorney General.
